# Employee Journey

> Make PLAYPLATE the best place to work in the category. The engine supports employees at
> every stage — fair, growth-oriented, wellbeing-first — under the hard rule that AI is
> **assistive, never authoritative**, and never the sole basis for a livelihood decision
> (Section 4 constitution).

---

## 1. Lifecycle overview

```
 APPLY ─▶ ONBOARD ─▶ GROW ─▶ THRIVE ─▶ LEAD ─▶ ALUMNI (fair offboarding)
   ▲                        │
   └──── RE-ENGAGE ◀── (support before burnout/exit) ────┘
```

## 2. Stage-by-stage

### 2.1 Apply & hire
- **Engine role:** support fair, structured hiring (consistent criteria, bias-aware).
- **Guardrail 🔒:** AI may assist scheduling/logistics and surface structured info; **humans
  make hiring decisions**; no sensitive-attribute inference; fairness-audited.

### 2.2 Onboard
- **Employee goal:** feel welcomed, learn fast, know what's expected.
- **Engine role:** personalised learning path (LMS), buddy/mentor pairing, clear
  transparency about *what the wellbeing system does and their rights* (Section 4.7).
- **Emotion target:** welcome, clarity, safety.

### 2.3 Grow
- **Employee goal:** build skills, see a future.
- **Engine role:** micro-training in flow (teachable moments), visible skills→certs→roles
  path (gamified healthily, 1.13), next-step recommendations.
- **Guardrail:** growth is opportunity, never pressure; suggestions, not mandates.

### 2.4 Thrive (wellbeing-first)
- **Employee goal:** fair schedule, manageable load, be recognised.
- **Engine role:**
  - **Fair scheduling** (Section 4.5): equitable shifts, honoured preferences, adequate
    rest, predictability.
  - **Burnout early-warning** (Section 4.4): the employee sees their *own* risk first;
    elevated risk routes to **relief and care**.
  - **Recognition surfacing**: ensures good work — especially the quiet kind — is *seen*.
- **Emotion target:** respected, supported, valued (which transmits to guests, 1.16).
- **Guardrail 🔒:** wellbeing data is the employee's; managers see *support flags*, not raw
  private notes; short retention.

### 2.5 Lead
- **Employee goal:** more responsibility, mentorship, impact.
- **Engine role:** surface leadership potential (initiative, reliability, peer trust,
  mentoring) for a development track and shift-lead opportunities.
- **Guardrail:** potential signals *inform* human development decisions; never auto-promote/
  demote; fairness-audited.

### 2.6 Performance & review (human-led)
- **Engine role:** a **balanced scorecard** (quality, growth, reliability, teamwork, guest
  impact) as *input* to a human manager's holistic, contextual judgement — shown *with
  context* (staffing, sample size, abusive-guest flags).
- **Guardrail 🔒 (the line):** **AI never decides pay, discipline, promotion, or
  termination.** A documented human rationale is required and must stand on its own; the
  system refuses to emit "penalise this person" outputs (Section 4.8, 11.2).

### 2.7 Re-engage / retain
- **Engine role:** spot rising turnover risk from *fair* signals and prompt **supportive**
  manager action (a conversation, relief, growth opportunity) — before the person is lost.
- **Guardrail:** support, not surveillance; the goal is the employee's wellbeing, not just
  retention metrics.

### 2.8 Offboarding (fair, dignified)
- **Engine role:** smooth logistics, capture (voluntary) exit-reason themes to improve, keep
  the door open (alumni/re-hire).
- **Guardrail:** dignity; data minimisation and retention rules apply (Section 11.7).

## 3. A single shift — the micro-journey

```
 PRE-SHIFT   ─ clear, fair, predictable schedule; know the forecasted load
 CHECK-IN    ─ optional wellbeing check-in (voluntary); protected break scheduled
 ON-FLOOR    ─ engine assists: prep guidance, recovery alerts, staffing balance
 SUPPORT     ─ break nudges honoured; understaffing flagged & fixed; recognition given
 WRAP        ─ kudos exchanged; growth nudge if a teachable moment happened
 AFTER       ─ fair-hours tracking; burnout signals feed *support*, not judgement
```

---

## 4. The manager's role (and accountability)

The engine makes managers *better and fairer*, and holds them accountable:
- It surfaces who needs support, who deserves recognition, and where schedules are unfair.
- It measures whether managers **act on** burnout flags and recognition prompts (Section
  9.6 HR dashboard) — closing the loop so "support" isn't just a dashboard number.
- Manager SOPs (delivery/sops.md) train the *supportive* interpretation of every flag.

---

## 5. Why this is also a business strategy

Happy, fairly-treated, growing employees → warm, genuine service → emotional contagion to
guests (mirror neurons, 1.16) → guest happiness and loyalty (the whole rest of this bible).
The employee journey isn't a cost centre or a compliance chore — it's the **upstream source
of the guest experience** everything else optimises. Treating people well is the strategy.
