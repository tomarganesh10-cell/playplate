# Customer Journey

> The end-to-end guest experience, stage by stage — each mapped to the psychology (Section
> 1), the engine's role, the emotional target, and the guardrail. Designed for the peak-end
> rule: engineer the peak and the ending of every visit (1.15, 1.29).

---

## 1. Lifecycle overview

```
 DISCOVER ─▶ FIRST VISIT ─▶ ONBOARD/CONSENT ─▶ REPEAT ─▶ LOYAL ─▶ CHAMPION
     ▲                                                                  │
     └───────────────── WIN-BACK (if lapsed, with care) ◀──────────────┘
```

## 2. Stage-by-stage

### 2.1 Discover
- **Guest goal:** find a fun place to eat + play.
- **Channels:** creator/UGC content, referrals, social, walk-by (the gaming floor as living
  display, 1.28).
- **Engine role:** authentic social proof (1.7 — *real* popularity), creator community,
  referral rewards (1.9).
- **Emotion target:** curiosity, "this looks like my kind of place" (Gen-Z authenticity,
  1.18).
- **Guardrail 🔒:** no fake scarcity/popularity; honest marketing.

### 2.2 First visit
- **Guest goal:** a great time with low friction.
- **Engine role:** frictionless order/book (Section 10), warm human + avatar welcome, whole-
  party staging (families, 1.20), a genuinely great core experience.
- **Emotion target:** delight, ease, belonging-begins (1.12).
- **Guardrail:** great baseline service **without** requiring any data/consent.

### 2.3 Onboard & consent (make privacy delightful) 🔒
- **Guest goal:** understand "what's in it for me" to opt in.
- **Engine role:** the avatar/app explains, in plain language, the *benefit* of remembering
  favourites and the *control* they keep (Section 2.5, 11.4). Consent is a pleasant,
  benefit-led choice, not a wall.
- **Emotion target:** trust ("they respect me").
- **Guardrail:** granular, revocable, no dark patterns; opting out costs nothing.

### 2.4 Repeat (build the habit)
- **Guest goal:** an easy, rewarding reason to come back.
- **Engine role:** the habit loop (1.10) — timed cues (within caps), one-tap "usual,"
  reliable + variable rewards (1.11), missions (Section 8), "for you" recs (Section 7).
- **Emotion target:** anticipation, competence, "this is *my* spot."
- **Guardrail:** healthy engagement, not compulsion; frequency caps; responsible-play.

### 2.5 Loyal (deepen)
- **Guest goal:** status, community, mastery.
- **Engine role:** tier progression, squads, tournaments, recognition, signature surprises
  (reciprocity + status, 1.9/1.13). Proactive *care* if churn-risk rises (Section 12).
- **Emotion target:** pride, belonging, attachment (oxytocin, 1.12).
- **Guardrail:** loyalty rewards are additive delights, never gates on basic service;
  generous, forgiving tiers.

### 2.6 Champion (advocacy)
- **Guest goal:** express identity, share, create, belong at the top.
- **Engine role:** creator tier, co-marketing, community leadership, referrals — turning top
  guests into brand engines (brand affinity, Section 2).
- **Emotion target:** identity, meaning, status.
- **Guardrail:** authentic; never exploit advocates; fair value exchange.

### 2.7 Win-back (with care, not pressure)
- **Guest goal:** a warm reason to return.
- **Engine role:** churn-risk triggers a *caring* re-engagement — "we miss you," a genuine
  gift (1.9), a new season as a natural re-entry (Section 8.5).
- **Emotion target:** feeling remembered and valued.
- **Guardrail 🔒:** care, not guilt or pressure; easy to stay opted-out.

## 3. A single visit — the micro-journey (peak-end engineered)

```
 ARRIVE      ─ warm welcome (human + avatar), recognised, station/settings pre-warmed
 SETTLE      ─ frictionless order/book; "for you" (easy-decline); comfortable staging
 EXPERIENCE  ─ flow-matched play (1.21), well-timed food/drink, shared fun (squads)
 PEAK    ★   ─ engineered highlight: a win celebrated, a surprise reward, a hype moment
 RESOLVE     ─ smooth pay (tap/at-table), fair, honest, no surprises
 SEND-OFF ★  ─ warm goodbye, loyalty celebrate, "what's next" teaser, easy feedback ask
 AFTER       ─ closed-loop feedback follow-up; between-visit narrative (missions, events)
```

★ = the two moments the guest will remember most (peak-end, 1.15). The engine's job is to
make sure both land.

---

## 4. Emotion & recovery across the journey (Section 6)

At every stage the experience-sentiment radar watches (from *voluntary/operational* signals
only) for friction and **rushes to recover** (Section 3, 6): a long wait, a wrong order, a
lost game handled gracefully. A recovered failure, over-corrected with genuine generosity
(1.9), often creates *more* loyalty than a flawless visit — the engine treats recovery as a
first-class, human-led priority.

## 5. Personas (behavioural, non-sensitive) — how the journey flexes

| Persona (behavioural) | What they want | Journey emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Competitive Squad Gamer | Skill, competition, crew | Tournaments, squads, flow-match, hype peaks |
| Chill Coffee Regular | Calm, ritual, comfort | Wind-down zone, the "usual," gentle recs |
| Family Organiser | Everyone happy, easy, safe | Whole-party staging, birthday engine, trust |
| Explorer / Foodie | Novelty, discovery | Serendipity slots, new drops, missions |
| Creator | Status, identity, community | Creator tier, events, co-marketing |
| Casual First-Timer | Ease, welcome, low pressure | Guide avatar, onboarding, great baseline |

Personas are **behavioural archetypes** (Section 2.4), used to tailor emphasis — never to
infer or act on sensitive identity (Section 11).
